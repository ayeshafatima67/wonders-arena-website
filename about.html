About the club

A place for every age to play, train, compete and belong.

Our story

The Wonders Arena is a multi-sport club in F-8 Markaz, Islamabad, built for people of all ages.

Before this website, members learned about timings, plans and events from notice boards, phone calls and social media. We bring everything together so that you can plan your visit, compare plans and join the club from your phone or computer.

People join alone or with their family, and every person chooses their own sports.

Mission and what we offer

Our mission

To make sport easy, safe and enjoyable for every family in Islamabad, and to build a community where people train, compete and belong.

What we offer

  • Courts for squash, badminton, padel, tennis, basketball and volleyball
  • Swimming, golf, horse riding, boxing and yoga
  • A gym with or without an instructor
  • A jogging and racing ground and a cafe

Our coaches

Every sport has qualified coaches for kids and adults. Swimming, the gym and boxing have separate timings for women and men.

Events and competitions

Fitness classes, workshops and motivation lectures run in our big hall, and the club holds club and national competitions with prizes.
