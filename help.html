Help

Answers to common questions and a way to contact us.

Frequently asked questions

Q1. How do I join the club?

Open the Membership page, choose a plan and fill in the sign-up form. Club staff will contact you to complete your membership at the club.

Q2. What are the timings?

Each sport has its own timings. Open the Facilities page to see all timings, including separate women and men slots for swimming, the gym and boxing.

Q3. Is there a family plan?

Yes. The family plan covers up to 4 members and each member can choose different sports. Extra members can be added.

Q4. Are there coaches?

Yes. Every sport has coaches for kids and adults.

Q5. Can I book a court online?

Not on this website. Courts are booked at the club.

Q6. Can I buy equipment online?

No. Equipment such as swim costumes, racquets, balls and golf sets is bought at the club. Members of 3 or more sports get 50% off.

Q7. Do I need an account to look around?

No. Choose Continue as Guest to browse every information page without logging in.

Q8. How can I contact the club?

Use the contact form below, call the club or visit us in F-8 Markaz, Islamabad.
