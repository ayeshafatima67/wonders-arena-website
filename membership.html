Membership

Join alone or with your family. Each member picks their own sports.

Compare plans

Membership fees in Pakistani rupees (indicative)
Plan3 months6 months12 months
Single (1 person)Rs. 15,000Rs. 27,000Rs. 48,000
Family (up to 4 members)Rs. 36,000Rs. 66,000Rs. 120,000
  • Each member chooses their own sports. Two sports per member are included, extra sports cost Rs. 500 per month each.
  • Each extra family member (more than 4) costs Rs. 1,200 per month.
  • Gym: without an instructor Rs. 1,500 per month, with an instructor Rs. 3,000 per month, for each gym member.
  • Members of 3 or more sports get 50% off club equipment.

Fees shown are indicative. The club confirms the final fee when you join.

How the fee is worked out

  1. The plan and duration give the base fee from the table above.
  2. Family plans include 4 members. Extra members are added per month.
  3. Sports beyond the 2 included per member are added per month.
  4. The gym option is added for each member who uses the gym.

These fees are an estimate only. No payment is taken on this website.
